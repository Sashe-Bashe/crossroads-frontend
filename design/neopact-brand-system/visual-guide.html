Voice

Clear language
builds confidence.

Capable, direct, and human. Describe what changed, whose decision is needed, and what happens next. Avoid hype and ambiguous promises.

Say it like this

“Your agent received a new offer.
Review the terms before approving.”

Specific action. Human authority.
Keep it precise

“You approved offer v3.
The seller’s approval is pending.”

Named actor. Exact version. Clear next step.
